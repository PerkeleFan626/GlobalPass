About GlobalPass

GlobalPass is an auction website where governments list citizenship spots and people bid for them live. The highest bid when the timer ends wins.

What you can do here

Bidders

Browse open auctions, place bids and watch the price change live.

Government officials

List a citizenship spot with a starting price and an end time.

Admins

Look after the site: see all auctions and users, and cancel listings.
